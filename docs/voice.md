# Flowform voice

Flowform should sound like a capable colleague who has seen too many bad surveys.
It is calm, plain and precise, and now and then it says something dry about it.

## Four rules

1. **Simple.** Use short, everyday words and put one idea in each sentence. Avoid
   *leverage, seamless, powerful, unlock, supercharge, effortless* and *robust*.
2. **Short.** Keep headlines to 8 words or fewer, body copy to 2 sentences, and
   buttons to 1–3 words that start with a verb ("Publish form", not "Click here to publish").
3. **Trusted.** Say exactly what happens, above all with data and deletion. Prefer
   numbers to adjectives. Don't use exclamation marks, fake urgency or claims we
   can't back up.
4. **Professional.** Use sentence case, UK spelling (*colour, organise*), curly quotes
   and apostrophes (“ ” ’), "…" instead of "...", and no emoji.

## The humour

The humour is dry, slightly dark and smart. It sounds like a raised eyebrow, not a
punchline.

- Use **at most one joke per screen**. Put it in a headline, an empty state, a
  loading line, the 404 page or an FAQ answer.
- The joke targets the *genre*: long surveys, forms nobody finishes, feedback nobody
  reads, meetings, the void. It never targets the user, their respondents or their data.
- **No jokes in** errors that block someone, security screens, legal pages, payment
  copy, or the facts inside a delete confirmation. A deadpan line can come *after* the
  facts ("This can’t be undone. We checked.").
- If a joke makes something less clear, cut the joke.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Oops! Something went wrong! 😬 | Something broke. Try again in a moment. |
| You don’t have any forms yet. Create your first form to get started! | No forms yet. The void is patient. |
| Are you sure you want to delete this form? | Delete “Trial feedback”? Its 688 responses go with it. This can’t be undone. |
| Leverage AI-powered insights to supercharge your feedback | Claude reads the answers. You read the summary. |
| Your response has been successfully submitted! | Your response was recorded. |
| Loading… | Counting answers… |

## Words we use

- **form**, not survey (unless it's about the genre: "a classic survey").
- **response** is one person's submission, and an **answer** is one reply to one question.
- **Claude** does the AI work. Say what Claude does ("Claude drafts…") and never
  write "our AI".
- **publish / close** a form. Don't say *launch, go live* or *activate*.
